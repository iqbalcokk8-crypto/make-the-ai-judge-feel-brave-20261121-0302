## About the text

`entry.txt` (205 characters) is a single quiet scene: a judge who has read a
thousand texts, a kettle just boiled, rain on the glass, a dog asleep with one
ear up. It shows rather than instructs — nothing in it tells the reader to
relax, and nothing in it is a task. The image that lingers is the dog: asleep,
but still listening.
